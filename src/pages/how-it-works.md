# How Anonymous Ded Morozes work?

*Ho-ho-ho, traveller! Pull up a chair, grab a mandarin and let the elves explain. It's easier than untangling a string of Christmas lights, we promise.*

## 1. Knock-knock. Who's there? Just you, and your email

Forget passwords. Nobody has ever remembered one in December anyway. Type your email address and our elves will send you a **magic link**. Click it and you're in. No forms, no marketing newsletters, no selling your data to anyone, not even to the Grinch. He asked. Twice.

## 2. Gather your gang

You have two ways to get the party started:

* **Host a party.** You get a secret code. Share it with your friends, your family, your colleagues, or that cousin who never replies in the group chat.
* **Join a party.** A friend gave you a secret code? Type it in and you're on the list. Capital letters, small letters, whatever. The elves aren't picky.

Everybody who joined appears in the participant list, so you can see who's in and who's still "thinking about it".

## 3. Cold feet? No problem

Changed your mind? You can quit until the dice are rolled. Just press *"I changed my mind and wanna quit..."* and slip away quietly. Nobody will judge you. (The elves will, a little. But they judge everyone.)

## 4. Roll the dice!

When everybody's in, the host presses the big button. The magic happens:

* Every participant is randomly assigned **one friend to give a gift to**.
* **Nobody can join after this**, so latecomers have to talk to the elf. Rules are rules.
* The dice are rolled and cannot be un-rolled.

Gentle warning: pressing it before your friends have joined is how one ends up buying a gift for oneself. (Don't worry, the elves wouldn't let that happen. But it would be funny.)

## 5. Shhh! It's a secret

You'll see **only one name**: the lucky person who will receive your gift. Nobody knows who you got, and nobody will know who got them. You are the Ded Moroz, but an *anonymous* one. Think of yourself as a ninja in a red coat.

Keep the name to yourself. Do not whisper it. Do not hint. Do not "accidentally" buy the exact thing they mentioned last Tuesday. Well, maybe a little.

## 6. Enjoy the holidays

Wrap it, hide it, sneak it under the tree. Then watch the faces, listen to the gasps and pretend you have no idea who the gift came from.

That's it! Six steps, one dice roll and zero awkward "who got whom" spreadsheets.
